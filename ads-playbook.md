## The North Star

Every ad drives one action: contractor fills out the intake form at `intake.tractifyhq.com`. Or, for the warm traffic strategy, joins the Facebook group first — then converts to the form after trust is built.

**Rules that never change:**
- Never mention website, system, or technology — only booked jobs and outcomes
- The offer is always 5 free booked jobs, no strings
- Every ad ends with one CTA — the form or the group, never both

## Audience Ranking (Highest → Lowest Conversion Potential)

### 1. HomeAdvisor / Angi / Yelp Contractors ← SHARPEST AUDIENCE WE HAVE

**Why this is the best audience:**
These contractors already believe in paid acquisition — they're spending $300-800/month on HomeAdvisor right now. They don't need to be convinced the problem is worth paying to solve. They just need to be convinced Tractify solves it better. HomeAdvisor charges $15-80 per lead with no booking guarantee. At a 20% lead-to-job conversion rate, their effective cost per booked appointment is $75-400 — plus the time chasing leads who don't answer. Tractify delivers booked appointments at a flat monthly cost with zero chasing.

**Their specific pain (use this language in ads):**
- Leads that don't pick up
- Paying for leads that go to 3 other contractors simultaneously
- Spending Sunday evening calling back HomeAdvisor leads
- No guarantee any lead turns into an actual job
- Feeling like a telemarketer in their own business

**Facebook targeting stack:**
- Interests: "Angi," "HomeAdvisor," "Thumbtack," "Yelp for Business"
- Combined with: HVAC interests + small business owner behavior
- Geographic: Washington state to start, expand by market as conversion is proven

**Ad angles that hit this audience:**
- *"Still chasing HomeAdvisor leads that won't pick up? We deliver booked appointments — not leads. First 5 free."*
- *"Paying $50 per lead and converting 20%? There's a better way."*
- Split screen visual: left = missed call notification + HomeAdvisor lead saying "text me" and ghosting. Right = Tractify calendar with confirmed appointment.
- *"HomeAdvisor gives you leads. We give you booked appointments."* (simple, clean, devastating)

### 2. HVAC Contractors — Broad Cold Traffic

**Facebook targeting stack:**
- Job title: "HVAC technician," "HVAC contractor," "heating and cooling," "air conditioning"
- Combined with: business owner or self-employed behavior
- Geographic: Washington state to start

**Ad angles:**
- *"HVAC contractors — we'll book your first 5 jobs for free."*

### 5. Facebook Group Members — Warmest Non-Client Audience

Contractors who joined the group, engaged with content, watched win posts. They know Jose and Daniel, they've seen real results, they trust the brand. These convert at a significantly higher rate than cold traffic. The group is the trust layer that warms up cold audiences before asking them to fill out the form.

## Ad Creative Formula

**Structure every ad:**
1. Hook (first 2 seconds) — pain point they feel every single day
2. Body — show the solution working (screen recording of calendar filling, dashboard showing revenue)
3. CTA — "Claim your 5 free jobs" → intake.tractifyhq.com

**Content formats that convert:**
- Raw iPhone video: Jose or Daniel talking directly to camera. Real, no production.
- Screen recording: show a contractor calendar filling up with real appointments
- Case study breakdown: "Day 1 live. Day 2: first booking. Day 4: all 5 done." + portal screenshot
- Split screen: old way (missed calls, chasing leads) vs Tractify (booked appointments, calendar full)
- Dashboard screenshot ad: "Can your current marketing agency show you this screen?" with revenue dashboard

## The Facebook Group Strategy

**Group concept:** "Home Service Contractors — More Booked Jobs" (or similar non-niche-specific name)
Not HVAC-specific — applies to all home service niches as Tractify expands.

**Purpose:** Long-play acquisition channel. Build with genuine value. Become the best sales channel over time.

**Content formula for the group:**
- Tactical value posts: breakdown of how a contractor got jobs through a specific channel — no pitch, just value
- Question posts that generate conversation: "What's your biggest frustration with your current lead source?" — contractor comments become ad copy research
- Win posts: when a client gets their 5 jobs, post the data. Group members see it and ask how to sign up.
- Behind the scenes: Jose and Daniel building Tractify. People root for founders they can watch.

**The acquisition mechanic:**
Run the HomeAdvisor targeting ad but drive traffic to the group instead of directly to the intake form. Contractors join, get weeks of genuine value, watch win posts from real clients, and self-convert. People who come through the group convert at a higher rate than cold form traffic because the trust is already there.

**Timing:** Start building the group aggressively alongside content creation from day one of August. The group is a long play — every week it gets more valuable as the audience grows and the win posts accumulate.

## The Warm Traffic Funnel (Group → Form)

```
Facebook Ad (HomeAdvisor targeting)
        ↓
Join Facebook Group ("Home Service Contractors — More Booked Jobs")
        ↓
2-4 weeks of value content + win posts
        ↓
Contractor self-selects to intake.tractifyhq.com
        ↓
Form → inline booking → onboarding call (or self-serve checklist once built)
        ↓
Free trial → 5 jobs → conversion
```

Cold traffic → form = lower conversion
Cold traffic → group → form = higher conversion, lower cost per paying client

## The Automation Vision

As the playbook matures:
1. Build a swipe file of winning hooks, ad copy, and creative that convert
2. Document every targeting combination with its cost per form fill
3. Know which audience + creative + platform combination produces the best paying clients
